# How Does Unfollow Tracker Work?

Unfollow Tracker reads the data you already own — your official Instagram export — and shows you exactly who doesn't follow you back. There's no risk, it's completely free, and nothing you upload ever leaves your device. Just three steps:

## Request Your ZIP File

Go to your Instagram account settings, open **Meta Account**, then **Personal details**, then **Your Information and Permissions**, and choose **Export Your Information**. Select **Followers and following**, set the date range to **All time**, and choose **JSON** format (it's smaller and parses faster than HTML).

## Download the ZIP File

Instagram will notify you by email or in-app when the export is ready. Download the ZIP file to your device.

## Upload the ZIP File

Drop the file into the box above. Your browser reads it locally and shows your results in seconds — the file itself is never sent anywhere.

## Why Instagram Numbers Can Look Different Lately

On May 7, 2026, Instagram carried out a large cleanup of bot and inactive accounts — widely reported as the "Great Purge of 2026." Meta said the removals mainly affected automated and long-inactive profiles, not genuine active followers. A lower follower count isn't necessarily bad news: bots never liked, commented, or engaged in the first place, so your real audience now makes up a larger share of the total.

That said, real people unfollow accounts too, and Instagram never tells you who. Unfollow Tracker compares your followers and following lists directly, so you can see who left and decide who matters most to keep following.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How can I see who unfollowed me on Instagram?**
Upload your official Instagram data export ZIP to Unfollow Tracker. If you've saved an older export before, we'll show you exactly who left your followers list since then.

**Is Unfollow Tracker safe to use?**
Yes. You never log in or share your Instagram password. We only read the official export file Instagram gives you, and it's parsed entirely in your own browser.

**Can I check multiple accounts?**
Yes — export a ZIP for each account separately and analyze them one at a time.

**What's the difference between "not following back" and "unfollowed"?**
"Not following back" means someone you follow has never been on your followers list (or isn't currently). An "unfollower" is someone who *was* on an older followers list you saved but is missing from your newer one — meaning they actively stopped following you.

**Can I see who blocked me on Instagram?**
No. There's no reliable way to detect this from any app or tool, including this one. If someone has blocked you, their profile simply becomes inaccessible from your account.
