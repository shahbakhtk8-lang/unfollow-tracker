# What Are Instagram Highlights?

Instagram Highlights are saved collections of past Stories that a user pins permanently to their profile, right below their bio. Normally, a Story disappears after 24 hours, but when an account adds one to a Highlight, it stays visible for as long as the owner keeps it there. Many creators, brands, and public figures use Highlights almost like a mini portfolio, organizing their best content into labeled folders such as Travel, FAQ, or Reviews. Because Highlights sit on the profile itself, anyone who visits that profile can open and watch them without needing to catch a Story within its first day.

# Difference Between Stories and Highlights

A Story is temporary. It appears at the top of the Instagram app for 24 hours and then disappears unless the account owner saves it. A Highlight is that same Story content, selected by the owner and placed into a labeled circle on their profile, where it stays until they remove it themselves. In short, every Highlight started life as a Story, but not every Story becomes a Highlight.

# What Is a Highlight Cover

Each Highlight shows a round cover image on the profile, and that cover is usually just the first story inside it unless the account picked a custom image. Many brands design a matching set of covers so their profile looks organized and on-brand. When you use a Highlights viewer, the cover shown in the tool may differ slightly from the one on Instagram itself, since some tools display the first story rather than any custom artwork the account uploaded. This is normal and does not affect the actual content inside the Highlight.

**Can I view highlights from a private account?**

No. Highlights from a private account are only visible to the people that account has approved as followers, and this restriction comes from Instagram itself rather than from any third-party tool. **Is my identity or IP address tracked?**

**Is it legal to download Instagram Highlights?**

Viewing public content that an account has chosen to make visible to everyone is generally not an issue, since that's exactly what public means on Instagram. Downloading and reusing someone else's photos or videos without permission is a different matter, and can run into copyright concerns depending on how you use the file afterward. As a general rule, it's safest to only save content you have the rights to use, or that you plan to keep for personal viewing rather than reposting elsewhere.

**How long do Highlights stay available?**

Unlike a regular Story, which disappears after 24 hours, a Highlight stays on a profile for as long as the account owner keeps it there, which can be months or even years. The account can remove, rename, or edit a Highlight at any time, and there's no fixed expiration date set by Instagram itself. If you come back to search the same account later and a Highlight is missing, it most likely means the owner took it down.
