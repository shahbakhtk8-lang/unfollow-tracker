# Understanding Your Pending Follow Requests
A quick guide before and after you check your list

## Why Do You Have Pending Follow Requests?
Before using this tool, it helps to know what this list actually means. Here's a quick answer.

## How Our Tool Reads Your Pending Requests
When you export your data from Instagram as a ZIP file, it includes a record of every follow request you've sent that the other person hasn't accepted yet. Our tool reads that data directly from your ZIP file, with no manual digging through Instagram's activity log.

## Your Data Stays Safe in Your Browser
Many similar tools ask for your private information, like your username and password, but our tool works differently. We never ask for your login details, we never access your Instagram account, and your file never goes anywhere else. Everything is read directly in your browser.

## Why Some Requests Stay Pending for Years
Some people don't check their Instagram requests folder for months or even years. That doesn't mean they're ignoring you. Our tool sorts your requests by date and shows the oldest ones first, so the longest-pending requests are easy to spot.

## How to Act on Your Pending Requests
You can open your Instagram account and cancel the request yourself. We don't automate this step, because doing so would require your Instagram login, and that's exactly the kind of risk this tool is built to avoid. Instead, each entry has a "Cancel on Instagram" link that takes you straight to that person's profile, where you can withdraw the request in a couple of taps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long can a pending request stay unaccepted?**
There's no fixed time limit. Some Instagram users go a year or more without checking their requests folder at all.

**What happens when I click "Cancel on Instagram"?**
It takes you straight to that person's profile, where you can withdraw your follow request yourself.

**Can I check pending requests for multiple accounts?**
Yes. Just export a separate ZIP file for each account and upload them one at a time.

**Do I need to give my login credentials to check pending requests?**
No. We only read the official data file Instagram provides you. Your username and password are never needed.
